### SENTENCES ###

subject + verb.
I eat.
you drink.
he sleeps.
she runs.
we play.
you study.
they work.

subject + verb + object.
I eat the apple.
you drink the milk.
he reads the book.
she cooks the pasta.
we watch the television.
you listen to the music.
they carry the bags.

question + answer.
are you hungry? yes, I am hungry.
are you thirsty? no, I am not thirsty.
are you tired? yes, I am tired.
are you happy? yes, I am happy.
do you want to come? yes, I want to come.
can you help me? yes, I can help you.
do you have to go? yes, I have to go.

where + to be.
where are you? I am home.
where is the cat? it is in the garden.
where are the children? they are at school.
where is mom? she is in the kitchen.
where is the bread? it is on the table.

when + verb.
when do you eat? I eat at noon.
when do you sleep? I sleep at night.
when do you play? I play in the afternoon.
when do you study? I study in the morning.
when do you come? I come tomorrow.

why + verb.
why do you cry? I cry because I am sad.
why do you laugh? I laugh because I am happy.
why do you run? I run because I am in a hurry.
why do you eat? I eat because I am hungry.
why do you sleep? I sleep because I am tired.

we are tired but we go on.

I can come but I do not know where.
